Visual Modes

Mode 0
VU Meter
Classic bar that grows with volume. Green to red gradient. White peak-hold dot decays after each transient.
Mode 1
Pulse
All LEDs pulse in brightness with the beat. Hue drifts slowly so colour shifts even in silence.
Mode 2
Rainbow
Full-spectrum rainbow scrolls along the strip. Loud sounds accelerate the scroll speed.
Mode 3
Fire
Heat diffusion simulation. Sound injects heat at the base, driving taller and wilder flames.
